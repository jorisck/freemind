FAQ

Questions about this simple text editor for Mac.

Is Freemind a simple text editor for Mac?

Yes. Open the app and start typing: there is no document to create and no Save button, because everything is saved automatically as you type. There is no account and nothing to set up.

How is Freemind different from TextEdit?

TextEdit opens one document at a time. Freemind starts every day on an empty entry and keeps all your notes in a searchable history, with pins, projects and branches, so a pile of quick notes stays organized.

Where are my notes stored?

On your Mac, in the Documents/Freewrite folder, as plain Markdown (.md) files that any other app can open. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Does Freemind need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account, no analytics, and your notes never leave your computer.

Can I search inside my notes?

Yes. Search looks at what is written inside every note, not only at the titles.

Which Macs does Freemind run on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. The app is notarized by Apple.
